Editorial policy

How our guides are produced, what gets checked before publication, and how corrections work.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who produces the guides

CrochetGuides is edited by a small in-house editorial team. Guides are commissioned around the questions beginners actually ask, then drafted and reviewed against a shared style and accuracy checklist before they go live.

AI assistance and human review

We use AI tools to help draft guides and produce supporting illustrations. AI output is not published unchecked. A human editor reviews every guide for stitch maths, terminology, internal consistency and unsupported claims before publication, and can rewrite or reject any section.

Some guides use a first-person editorial voice for readability. Those first-person examples describe typical situations and are not reports that the editor personally tested every product or followed that exact sequence. Where advice is based on published specifications, standard construction methods or a sampled swatch, the guide says so. We do not invent named experts or credentials.

What we check

  • Stitch counts and repeats are consistent from the first round to the last.
  • US and UK terminology is not mixed, and the convention used is stated.
  • Hook size, yarn weight and gauge advice are compatible with the pattern.
  • Safety advice is included for toys, small parts, babies and items that will be washed.
  • Claims about products are limited to what can be supported by the manufacturer's specifications or visible evidence.
  • Internal links point to the live guide, not a redirected or retired URL.

Pattern accuracy and limits

Crochet outcomes vary with yarn, hook, tension and hand position. A pattern that works at the stated gauge can still need adjustment. We include gauge and stitch counts so you can check the work as you go, but we cannot guarantee that every reader will match the sample exactly.

Follow the safety notes on toys and items for babies. Small parts, long cords and loose fibres can be hazardous. Nothing on this site is medical, legal or professional advice.

Updates and corrections

Guides carry a review date. When we correct a published instruction, we update the page and add a short correction note at the end of the affected section. To report an error, email contact@crochetguide.uk with the URL and the instruction in question. We aim to reply within two working days.

Commercial independence

Advertising and affiliate links fund the site. They do not decide which products we recommend. Sponsored content, if it ever appears, is labelled as sponsored. Affiliate links are disclosed at the point of use and in the site footer.

Images

Illustrations may be AI-generated and are used to show construction or finished shape, not to document a specific tested sample. Where a photograph is used, it is credited or owned by CrochetGuides.

Contact

Questions about this policy, corrections, or a privacy request: contact@crochetguide.uk, or use the contact page.
