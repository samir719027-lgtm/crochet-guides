Contact

Contact CrochetGuides

Corrections, guide requests, privacy questions and commercial enquiries all reach the same small editorial team.

Email us

The quickest way to reach us is contact@crochetguide.uk. We aim to reply within two working days. CrochetGuides is an online-only publication; email is the most reliable contact route.

Reporting an error

Please include the guide URL and the instruction you think is wrong. If you can, tell us the round or section and what happened when you followed it. We check every report and add a correction note when we change a published instruction.

Guide requests

If a beginner question is not covered, send it over. We use reader questions to plan new guides, though we cannot promise to cover every request.

Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete personal data, email us with the subject line "Privacy request". Our privacy policy explains what we hold and how long we keep it.

Advertising and partnerships

We label sponsored content and do not sell positive reviews or placements inside editorial guides. For advertising enquiries, email us with the subject line "Advertising".
