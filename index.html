First skein to first stitch

Four steps before your first project

The order actually matters — skip step two and most beginners quit by step three.

Pick the right hook

Start with a 5.0mm (H) aluminum hook — forgiving grip, easy to see your stitches.

Choose beginner yarn

Light, smooth worsted-weight cotton or acrylic. Skip fuzzy or dark yarn at first — you won't see your stitches.

Learn two stitches

Chain stitch and single crochet cover most beginner patterns. Master those before anything fancier.

Make a swatch, not a project

A flat 20x20 square teaches tension without the pressure of finishing something wearable.
