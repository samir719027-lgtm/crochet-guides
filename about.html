About

Guides written for the person holding a hook for the first time.

We're a small team that got tired of crochet advice that assumes you already know what "hdc" means.

Why we started this

Most crochet content online is written by experienced crocheters, for other experienced crocheters. That's great if you already know the basics — and frustrating if you're the person trying to figure out why your first swatch looks nothing like the photo.

CrochetGuides exists to close that gap: plain-language guides that explain the "why" behind hook sizes, yarn weights, and pattern shorthand, not just the "what."

Our approach: every guide is written to answer the question a beginner actually has, in the order they actually need it — not the order that's easiest to write about.

How we pick products

When we recommend a hook, a skein of yarn, or a book, we're looking for one thing: would this genuinely help someone who's just starting out. We favour widely available, well-reviewed options over expensive or niche gear, because the biggest risk for a beginner isn't buying the "wrong" premium hook — it's spending too much before knowing if the craft is for them.
