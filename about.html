About

Plain-English crochet guides, made carefully.

CrochetGuides publishes beginner-first tutorials, free patterns and honest supply comparisons. This page explains who makes the guides, how they are checked, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

What this site is for

Most crochet advice is written for people who already know the shorthand. CrochetGuides starts earlier: what a stitch is, why the hook size matters, how to read a round, and what to do when the finished piece does not match the photo. The aim is a guide you can follow with the yarn in your hand, not a glossary you have to decode first.

How our guides are made

Every guide is drafted with AI assistance and then reviewed and edited by a human editor before publication. The editor checks stitch counts and repeats, confirms the terminology, and removes claims the site cannot support.

We do not pretend that every hook, yarn or pattern has been physically tested by the person writing. When a guide relies on published specifications, common practice or a sampled swatch, we say so. When something has not been tested, we do not describe it as tested.

Guides use a first-person editorial voice for readability. First-person examples describe typical situations and are not reports that the editor personally tested a product or followed that exact sequence.

Our rule: if we cannot explain why an instruction should work, it does not go on the page.

What we check before publishing

  • Stitch counts add up across increase, straight and decrease rounds.
  • Terminology is consistent and stated as US or UK terms.
  • Yarn weight, hook size and gauge advice match the construction described.
  • Safety notes are included for toys, small parts and items for babies.
  • Product claims are limited to specifications and limitations we can verify.

How we choose products

Recommendations are based on published specifications, price, availability and the needs of a beginner. We favour widely available tools over niche premium gear, because the biggest risk for a new crocheter is spending too much before knowing whether the craft will stick. We do not accept payment for a placement or a positive review.

Who we are

CrochetGuides is an independent online publication run by a small editorial team. We are not a retailer and we do not sell yarn, hooks or finished items directly. The site is supported by advertising and affiliate links, which are labelled where they appear.

Corrections and contact

If you spot an error, email contact@crochetguide.uk with the guide URL and the instruction you think is wrong. We aim to reply within two working days. When we change a published instruction, we add a correction note to the guide so readers can see what changed.
