If you contact us by email, we'll have whatever information you choose to include in that message, used only to respond to you.

If you're in the UK or EU, you have the right to ask what data we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, and withdraw consent for anything you previously opted into (such as the email list). Contact us using the details below to exercise any of these rights.
