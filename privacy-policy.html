Privacy policy

What data this site collects, how cookies and advertising work, and how to exercise your rights.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

CrochetGuides (crochetguide.uk) is an independent crochet publication and the data controller for this website. For any privacy question or request, email contact@crochetguide.uk.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. If you request a PDF from our library, we receive the email address you enter and the page you requested it from.
  • Analytics data. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 records pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source and interaction events. We use this to understand which guides are useful.
  • Advertising data. Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to measure and personalise ads, subject to your choices and to Google's own consent requirements.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs, including IP address, user agent and requested URL, for security and abuse prevention.

Legal bases

We rely on your consent for analytics and personalised advertising; on legitimate interests for keeping the site secure, preventing abuse and understanding aggregate usage; and on the performance of a request when we send you a PDF or reply to your email.

Cookies we use

  • cg-consent (local storage): remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies. It stays in your browser until you clear it.
  • _ga, _ga_* (Google Analytics): distinguishes users and sessions. Set only after you accept analytics.
  • __gads, __gpi, IDE, test_cookie (Google advertising): used by Google to measure ad performance and, where permitted, personalise ads. The advertising script is not loaded until you accept.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time. Blocking them may affect how ads and analytics work, but the guides remain readable.

Advertising and Google

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings, and learn about other opt-out tools at aboutads.info and Your Online Choices. Where required, we ask for your consent before loading analytics or advertising scripts, and ads are not personalised if you reject. You can change your choice at any time using the link in the footer.

Who we share data with

We share data with service providers that help us run the site: Google (analytics and advertising), our email delivery provider for PDF requests, and our hosting provider. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the site and its readers.

International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK. Where that happens, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Email correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve your query and meet legal obligations. PDF request emails are kept until the request is fulfilled and then removed from active storage. Server logs are kept for a short security period.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise a right, email contact@crochetguide.uk.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If you are in another country, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use HTTPS across the site and restrict access to the services that hold data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our tools or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact@crochetguide.uk. See also our terms of use and editorial policy.
