Free Crochet Kids Fingerless Mitts Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A simple tube with a thumb gap — quick enough to finish a pair in one sitting.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Ages 3–8, adjustable

These mitts are a plain tube worked in a spiral, the same continuous-round technique from our beanie pattern, with a small gap left for the thumb. No fitted shaping, no fingers to work individually — just a snug tube that stretches over a child's hand.

Materials

  • Small amount of worsted-weight yarn (about 50–70 yards for a pair)
  • 4.5mm (US size 7) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
rnd(s)Round(s)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 4.5mm hook and worsted yarn. Fit depends directly on your starting chain — measure the child's hand before starting for the best fit.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral — use a stitch marker to track round starts, same technique as our beanie pattern.
  • Makes one mitt; repeat for a matching pair.
  • Measure around the child's hand (excluding thumb) for the starting chain — the numbers below fit an average 5–6 year old hand as a starting point.

Pattern (make 2)

Round 1

Ch 20, join with a sl st to form a ring, being careful not to twist the chain. Place marker. (20 sts)

Rounds 2–5

Sc in every st around. (20 sts each round)

Round 6: thumb gap

Sc 16, ch 4 (skip next 4 sts, leaving a gap for the thumb), sc in remaining sts to end of round. (20 sts total, including the 4-chain gap)

Round 7

Sc in every st around, working sc into each of the 4 chain sts from the gap as if they were regular stitches. (20 sts)

Rounds 8–10

Sc in every st around. (20 sts each round)

Finishing

Fasten off and weave in ends with a yarn needle. Repeat the entire pattern for the second mitt.

Frequently asked questions

How do I size fingerless mitts for a child's hand?

Measure around the widest part of the child's hand (excluding the thumb) and work enough rounds in the tube to match that circumference, adjusting the starting chain up or down in multiples of 2.

How much yarn do I need for a pair of kids mitts?

A pair of child-sized fingerless mitts uses roughly 50 to 70 yards of worsted-weight yarn total, comfortably within a single skein.

Where does the thumb hole go on fingerless mitts?

The thumb hole is created by skipping a small group of stitches partway up the tube rather than working them, leaving a gap. Chain over the gap on the return round to keep the stitch count consistent.
