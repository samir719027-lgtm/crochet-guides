Easy Crochet Phone Case Pattern (Custom Fit, Any Phone)

Pin this pattern

A snug, ribbed sleeve sized to your exact phone — no chart needed, just your tape measure.

Updated 2026 · US terms · 20 min read

This case is a single ribbed rectangle, folded in half and seamed up the sides, sized directly from your phone's measurements plus a small seam allowance.

Quick answer: Work a rectangle in half double crochet through the back loop only for a stretchy, ribbed texture, sized to your phone, then fold and seam the sides.

What you need

  • A small amount of worsted weight yarn
  • 3.5–4.0mm hook (a size smaller than the yarn label, for a snug, dense fabric)
  • Yarn needle
  • Optional: 1 button, for a flap closure

Step 1: measure and swatch

Steps

  1. Measure your phone's width and height (with its case on, if you use one).
  2. Work a small swatch in hdc, working every row through the back loop only, and measure your stitch and row gauge.
  3. Calculate your starting chain: (phone width × 2) + 1–2cm ease, converted to stitches using your gauge.

Step 2: work the body

Steps

  1. Chain the number calculated above, plus 2 turning chains.
  2. Row 1: hdc in the 3rd chain from the hook and each chain across, through both loops.
  3. Row 2 onward: chain 2, turn, hdc in the back loop only of each stitch across.
  4. Repeat until the piece measures (phone height × 2) + 2–3cm, then fasten off.

Step 3: fold and seam

Steps

  1. Fold the rectangle in half widthwise, right sides together.
  2. Seam up both sides, leaving the top open for the phone to slide in.
  3. Turn right side out.

Optional: flap and button closure

For a top flap, leave one side unclosed at the top instead — continue a few extra rows on the back panel only, shape a point or rounded flap, add a buttonhole (a small chain space in the last row) or a button loop, and stitch a matching button to the front panel.

Frequently asked questions

Why work through the back loop only?

It creates a stretchy, ribbed texture, similar to a knit rib, that hugs the phone's shape and adds grip, instead of a flat, loose fabric.

Will this fit any size phone?

Yes, since it's built from your own measurements rather than a fixed size — just recalculate the chain and row count for a different phone.

Can I felt this for extra padding?

Yes, if using 100% non-superwash wool, a gentle hot-wash felting process will shrink and thicken the fabric for a padded case.
