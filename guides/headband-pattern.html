Free Crochet Twisted Headband Pattern for Beginners

A single strip of half double crochet, twisted once and sewn closed — no shaping, no counting increases.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Adjustable for adult or child sizing

This headband is a single rectangular strip — the same flat-row technique from our scarf pattern — joined into a loop with one twist for shape. It's one of the fastest wearable projects on the site, and resizes for a child just by adjusting the starting chain.

Materials

  • 1 skein worsted-weight yarn (about 60–90 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for the twist seam and weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
hdcHalf double crochet

Gauge

Exact gauge isn't critical — the finished circumference depends entirely on your starting chain length, which you measure against the wearer's head before starting.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked flat in rows — same base technique as our scarf pattern.
  • Measure the wearer's head circumference before starting and chain to match; this is the only adjustment needed to resize for an adult or a child.
  • The video below shows the twisting and seaming step, which is easier to follow visually than in text alone.

Pattern

Foundation

Chain to match the wearer's head circumference plus 1in / 2.5cm for seam allowance, plus 2 for turning. For an average adult, this is roughly ch 24; for a child, roughly ch 20. Adjust to your own measurement.

Row 1

Hdc in the 3rd chain from hook, and in each chain across. Turn. (matches your starting chain minus 2)

Rows 2–6

Ch 2 (counts as first hdc), hdc in each stitch across. Turn. Repeat until the strip measures about 3–3.5in / 7.5–9cm wide (5 rows total for most yarn weights).

Finishing: twist and seam

Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Lay the strip flat, give one end a single half-twist, then bring the two short ends together with the twist in the middle. Whip stitch the short ends together securely with the tail and a yarn needle.

Video walkthrough of a similar twisted headband technique — useful for seeing the twist-and-seam step in motion.

If the twist won't stay in place: make sure the seam is worked tightly and the whip stitches are close together. A loosely seamed twist can shift out of position with wear.

Frequently asked questions

How much yarn do I need for a crochet headband?

A single adult headband uses roughly 60 to 90 yards of worsted-weight yarn, comfortably within a single skein with plenty left over.

How do I resize this headband for a child?

Chain to fit the child's head measurement instead of the adult length given in the pattern, keeping the row count the same. A shorter starting chain is the only change needed.

Why does my headband come out too loose or too tight?

The starting chain length determines the finished circumference directly, since this pattern has no shaping. Measure the wearer's head before starting and adjust the chain length up or down rather than changing hook size.
