Free Crochet Toddler Beanie Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A scaled-down version of our adult beanie — same technique, sized for a small head.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Ages 1–3, about 18–19in / 46–48cm head circumference

This is a direct scale-down of our adult beanie pattern, stopping the crown increases two rounds earlier and working fewer straight rounds for a smaller circumference. If you've already made the adult version, the technique here is identical.

Materials

  • 1 skein worsted-weight yarn (about 100–120 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
inc2 sc in same stitch (increase)
rnd(s)Round(s)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 5.0mm hook and worsted yarn. Fit depends more on gauge at this size than in an adult beanie — measure the toddler's head before starting if possible.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral — use a stitch marker to track round starts, same as our adult beanie pattern.
  • Toddler head sizes vary more than adult sizes. Measure and compare against the crown circle before starting the straight rounds if an exact fit matters.
  • This pattern stops crown increases at round 6 (adult version continues to round 8), which is the main difference between the two sizes.

Pattern

Round 1

Make a magic ring, 6 sc into ring, place marker. (6 sts)

Round 2

Inc in every st around. (12 sts)

Round 3

[sc 1, inc] around. (18 sts)

Round 4

[sc 2, inc] around. (24 sts)

Round 5

[sc 3, inc] around. (30 sts)

Round 6

[sc 4, inc] around. (36 sts)

Rounds 7–14

Sc in every st around, no increases. Work 8 rounds straight, or until the hat measures about 1.5–2in / 4–5cm less than the toddler's head circumference — single crochet fabric stretches slightly once worn.

Finishing

Slip stitch into the next stitch to close, fasten off leaving a 6in / 15cm tail. Weave in ends with a yarn needle.

Frequently asked questions

How do I scale an adult beanie pattern down for a toddler?

Stop the crown increase rounds two rounds earlier than an adult version, and work fewer straight rounds for the body, checking the flat crown circle against the toddler's head measurement as you go.

What head size does this toddler beanie fit?

This pattern is sized for an average 1 to 3 year old head circumference, roughly 18 to 19 inches. Measure the toddler's head for the most accurate fit, since sizes vary.

Is single crochet fabric warm enough for a toddler beanie?

Yes, single crochet produces a dense, tightly-worked fabric that blocks wind well. For extra warmth in very cold climates, a wool or wool-blend yarn adds insulation that acrylic doesn't provide.
