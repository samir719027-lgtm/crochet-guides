Free Crochet Coasters Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A quick round coaster using the same magic ring technique from our beanie pattern, sized down to fit a mug.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Makes a set of 4, about 4in / 10cm each

These coasters are a small flat circle worked in rounds, using the same increase logic as our beanie pattern crown — just stopped much earlier. Each one takes about 15 minutes once you're comfortable with the stitch, making a set of four a single-evening project.

Materials

  • 1 skein worsted-weight cotton yarn (about 60–80 yards for a set of 4)
  • 4.5mm (US size 7) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
inc2 sc in same stitch (increase)
rnd(s)Round(s)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 4.5mm hook and worsted cotton. Gauge isn't critical here — a coaster a little larger or smaller still works fine under a mug.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral — same technique as our beanie pattern. Use a stitch marker to track round starts.
  • Makes one coaster; repeat 3 more times for a set of four.
  • Cotton yarn is recommended over acrylic since it absorbs moisture better under a cold or warm drink.

Pattern (make 4)

Round 1

Make a magic ring, 6 sc into ring, place marker. (6 sts)

Round 2

Inc in every st around. (12 sts)

Round 3

[sc 1, inc] around. (18 sts)

Round 4

[sc 2, inc] around. (24 sts)

Round 5

[sc 3, inc] around. (30 sts)

Round 6: edge

Sl st into next st to close, or work one round of sc without increasing for a slightly firmer edge before closing.

Finishing

Fasten off, leaving a 4in / 10cm tail. Weave in ends with a yarn needle. Repeat the entire pattern 3 more times for a set of four.

If your circle cups or ruffles: too many or too few increases were worked in a round. Recount stitches after each round and compare to the numbers above before continuing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best yarn for crochet coasters?

Cotton worsted-weight yarn is the standard choice, since it absorbs moisture and holds its shape under a warm or cold drink, unlike acrylic which can flatten and stain more easily.

How big should a crochet coaster be?

A standard coaster measures about 4 inches (10cm) across, large enough for most mugs and glasses without extending far past the base of the cup.

How much yarn do I need for a set of coasters?

A set of four coasters uses roughly 60-80 yards of worsted-weight cotton yarn total, comfortably within a single skein with plenty left over.
