Crochet Christmas Stocking Pattern

Pin this pattern

A cozy, textured stocking worked flat in two pieces, with a cable-look cuff and a sturdy hanging loop.

Updated 2026 · US terms · 55 min read

This stocking is worked as two matching panels in half double crochet, seamed together, then finished with a contrast ribbed cuff and a hanging loop. The body uses the half double crochet in the third loop (hdc3lo) for a subtle knit-like texture, but regular hdc works fine too if you'd rather keep it simple.

Quick answer: Crochet two identical stocking-shaped panels, seam around the edges leaving the top open, then add a folded-over ribbed cuff and a hanging loop at the heel side.

What you need

  • Worsted weight (#4) yarn: main color (cream/off-white) and contrast color (red or green), about 150–180 yards total
  • 5.0mm (US H/8) hook
  • Stitch markers
  • Yarn needle
  • Optional: piece of cardboard or a printed template to keep both panels the same size

Panel shaping (make 2)

Steps

  1. Chain 22 in main color. Work hdc in the 2nd chain from hook and each chain across (21 sts). Chain 1, turn.
  2. Work even in hdc (or hdc3lo for texture) for 10 rows, keeping 21 sts in every row.
  3. Begin the foot shaping: at the start of the next row, work 2 sc into the first stitch to start rounding the heel corner, then continue in pattern to the end. Repeat this small increase every other row for 4 rows.
  4. For the toe, decrease 1 stitch at the start and end of every row for 6 rows (hdc2tog at each end) until about 9 sts remain.
  5. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for seaming.

Assembly

Steps

  1. Lay the two panels together, right sides facing.
  2. Using the yarn tail and a whip stitch or slip stitch seam, join around the toe, foot, and heel curve, leaving the entire top (ankle) edge open.
  3. Turn right side out.

Cuff and hanging loop

Steps

  1. Join contrast color at the top edge. Work 1 round of sc evenly around the opening to even out the stitch count, join with a slip stitch.
  2. Work the cuff in rows of front-post/back-post double crochet (or simple hdc if you prefer a plain cuff) for 5–6 rows, to about 3 inches tall.
  3. Fold the cuff down over the top of the stocking.
  4. Chain 12–15 at the top back seam, slip stitch to the top edge to form a hanging loop, then fasten off and weave in all ends.

Sizing note

This pattern makes a stocking roughly 16 inches from cuff to toe — a classic mantel size. For a larger stocking, start with a longer foundation chain and add extra rows before shaping the foot; for a mini ornament-sized version, halve the chain and row counts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make this without shaping the foot?

Yes — a simple rectangle folded in half and seamed on two sides makes an easier, boxier stocking that still hangs well.

What yarn weight works best?

Worsted (#4) gives a sturdy, huggable stocking. A chunkier yarn with a larger hook works too and finishes faster, but uses more yardage.

How do I keep both panels the same size?

Count your stitches and rows carefully, or lay the first panel flat as a guide while making the second.
