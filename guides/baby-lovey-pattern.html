Free Crochet Baby Lovey Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A small comfort blanket with a soft amigurumi head, sized to be easy for a baby to hold.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Blanket about 10in / 25cm square

This lovey combines a small flat square, using the same technique as our dishcloth pattern, with a soft amigurumi head from our mini ball pattern sewn onto one corner. Two familiar techniques combined into one genuinely useful baby gift.

Baby safety note: loveys are generally intended for babies with head and neck control (commonly from around 3 months), not newborns, and should be used according to current safe sleep guidance. Embroider the face with yarn rather than using plastic safety eyes for full child safety. See the FAQ below.

Materials

  • 1 skein soft, machine-washable worsted-weight yarn (about 80–100 yards)
  • 4.5mm (US size 7) crochet hook for the blanket square
  • 3.5mm hook for the amigurumi head
  • A small amount of polyester fiberfill stuffing
  • Embroidery thread, for a safe stitched face
  • Stitch marker, scissors, and a yarn needle

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 4.5mm hook and worsted yarn. Gauge isn't critical for the blanket square, though tighter tension on the head keeps the stuffing from showing.

Notes

  • Written in US terms. The blanket square uses the flat technique from our dishcloth pattern; the head uses the amigurumi technique from our mini ball pattern.
  • Use soft, machine-washable yarn labelled safe for babies.
  • Embroider the face rather than using safety eyes for a fully child-safe finish.

Pattern

Blanket square, Foundation

Chain 36.

Blanket square, Row 1

Single crochet in the 2nd chain from the hook, and in each chain across. Turn. (35 sts)

Blanket square, Rows 2–35

Chain 1, single crochet in each stitch across. Turn. (35 sts each row)

Repeat until the piece is square, roughly 35 rows for this gauge. Fasten off and weave in ends.

Head, Round 1

Using the 3.5mm hook, make a magic ring, 6 sc into ring, place marker. (6 sts)

Head, Round 2

Inc in every st around. (12 sts)

Head, Round 3

[sc 1, inc] around. (18 sts)

Head, Rounds 4–7

Sc in every st around, no shaping. (18 sts each round)

Head, Round 8

[sc 1, dec] around. (12 sts)

Begin stuffing firmly.

Head, Round 9

Dec in every st around, adding final stuffing. (6 sts)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

Assembly

Embroider a simple face onto the head with embroidery thread. Sew the head securely onto the centre of one edge of the blanket square, using small, tight stitches through several stitches on both pieces.

A demonstration of crocheting a baby lovey with an animal head attached to a blanket square, useful for seeing how the two pieces are joined.

Securing the head firmly: since babies handle loveys frequently, sew the head on with several passes of yarn through both layers, and check the attachment periodically for wear over time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a baby lovey?

A lovey is a small comfort item combining a soft square of blanket fabric with a small stuffed head or animal shape attached, sized to be easy for a baby to hold and carry.

Is a crochet lovey safe for a newborn?

Loveys and other soft toys are generally recommended for babies old enough to have some head and neck control, typically from around 3 months, rather than newborns, and should always follow current safe sleep guidance from a pediatrician.

How do I attach the head to the blanket square on a lovey?

Sew the head securely to the centre of one edge of the blanket square using the tail left from finishing the head, going through several stitches on both pieces rather than just a few anchor points.
