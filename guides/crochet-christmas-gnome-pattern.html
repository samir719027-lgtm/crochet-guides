Crochet Christmas Gnome Pattern

Pin this pattern

A no-face amigurumi gnome with a tall pointed hat and a soft yarn beard — a fast holiday shelf-sitter.

Updated 2026 · US terms · 50 min read

This gnome is built as one continuous cone-shaped body worked in the round, topped with a separate pointed hat, and finished with a hand-tied yarn beard — no face to embroider, which makes it a fast, forgiving amigurumi project. If amigurumi shaping is new to you, our amigurumi for beginners guide covers the core techniques first.

Quick answer: Crochet a cone-shaped body from a magic ring, stuff it, add a separate pointed hat worked the same way, and finish by tying strands of cream yarn into a beard between the hat and the body.

What you need

  • Worsted weight (#4) yarn: red or another festive color (body/hat) and cream (beard)
  • 4.0mm (US G/6) hook
  • Polyester fiberfill stuffing
  • Yarn needle
  • Optional: small wooden bead or pom-pom for the nose

Body

Steps

  1. Magic ring, 6 sc into the ring. (6 sts)
  2. Round 2: 2 sc in each st around. (12 sts)
  3. Round 3: *sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (18 sts)
  4. Round 4: *2 sc, 2 sc in next st* around. (24 sts)
  5. Rounds 5–16 (approx.): work even in sc without further increasing, so the tube widens visually into a cone as it's stuffed — stuff firmly as you go.
  6. Final round: sc2tog evenly around to close the bottom slightly, then fasten off and weave the tail through the remaining stitches to close.

Hat

Steps

  1. Magic ring, 6 sc into the ring. (6 sts)
  2. Increase the same way as the body for 3–4 rounds until the hat brim is wide enough to sit on top of the body.
  3. Work even, without further increasing, for 8–10 rounds so the hat narrows into a point relative to the body width, decreasing by 1–2 sts every couple of rounds near the very top to close it off neatly.
  4. Fasten off, leaving a tail for attaching.

Assembly

Steps

  1. Place the hat on top of the body, angled slightly if you like, and stitch it in place around the brim.
  2. Cut 12–15 strands of cream yarn, each about 4 inches long. Fold them in half and attach them in a row just under the hat brim using a lark's head knot or by stitching through, to form the beard.
  3. Trim the beard ends level, or leave them slightly uneven for a shaggier look.
  4. Optional: glue or stitch a small bead or pom-pom nose between the hat and beard.

Frequently asked questions

Do gnomes need a face?

No — the classic Scandinavian-style gnome has no visible face, just a hat and a beard, which is part of what makes this pattern quick to finish.

What can I use to weigh the base so it stands up?

A few tablespoons of poly pellets or dried rice sealed in a small bag, added to the bottom of the body before stuffing the rest with fiberfill.

Can I make a whole family of gnomes?

Yes — this pattern scales easily with a smaller hook and thinner yarn for mini versions, or a chunkier yarn for an oversized doorstop gnome.
