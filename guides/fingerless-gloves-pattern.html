Free Crochet Fingerless Gloves Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A snug tube with a thumb gap, sized for adult hands — the grown-up version of our kids mitts.

Updated 2026 · US terms · One adult size, adjustable

These gloves use the same tube-with-a-thumb-gap construction as our kids fingerless mitts pattern, scaled up for an adult hand and wrist. No fitted fingers to work individually — just a snug tube that stretches comfortably into place.

Materials

  • 1 skein worsted-weight yarn (about 100–130 yards for a pair)
  • 4.5mm (US size 7) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
rnd(s)Round(s)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 4.5mm hook and worsted yarn. Fit depends directly on your starting chain — measure your hand before starting for the best fit.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral — use a stitch marker to track round starts, same technique as our kids mitts pattern.
  • Makes one glove; repeat for a matching pair.
  • The numbers below fit an average adult hand; adjust the starting chain in multiples of 2 for a snugger or looser fit.

Pattern (make 2)

Round 1

Ch 28, join with a sl st to form a ring, being careful not to twist the chain. Place marker. (28 sts)

Rounds 2–7

Sc in every st around. (28 sts each round)

Round 8: thumb gap

Sc 22, ch 6 (skip next 6 sts, leaving a gap for the thumb), sc in remaining sts to end of round. (28 sts total, including the 6-chain gap)

Round 9

Sc in every st around, working sc into each of the 6 chain sts from the gap as if they were regular stitches. (28 sts)

Rounds 10–13

Sc in every st around. (28 sts each round)

Finishing

Fasten off and weave in ends with a yarn needle. Repeat the entire pattern for the second glove.

Frequently asked questions

How do I size fingerless gloves for my hand?

Measure around the widest part of your hand excluding the thumb, and adjust the starting chain up or down in multiples of 2 to match, keeping in mind single crochet fabric stretches slightly once worn.

How much yarn do I need for fingerless gloves?

A pair of adult fingerless gloves uses roughly 100 to 130 yards of worsted-weight yarn total, comfortably within a single skein.

What is the difference between these gloves and the kids fingerless mitts pattern?

The construction is identical, just scaled up: this version uses a larger starting chain and more rounds to fit an adult hand and wrist length.
