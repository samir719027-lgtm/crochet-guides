Free Crochet Slippers Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A cozy sole-and-sides construction, built from the same flat circle and straight rounds used across our beginner patterns.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Adult medium, adjustable

These slippers use the same oval-sole-plus-straight-sides technique from our baby booties pattern, scaled up for an adult foot. No fitted shaping around toes or heel — just a snug, warm shape you can make in an evening.

Materials

  • 2 skeins worsted-weight yarn (about 150–180 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends
  • Optional: fabric paint or suede sole patches for grip

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
inc2 sc in same stitch (increase)
rnd(s)Round(s)

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 5.0mm hook and worsted yarn. Fit depends directly on the sole chain length — measure the wearer's foot before starting for the best result.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral — use a stitch marker to track round starts, same technique as our baby booties pattern.
  • Makes one slipper; repeat the entire pattern once more for a matching pair.
  • For a firmer, warmer sole, work the first sole round through the back loop only, then continue as written.

Pattern (make 2)

Sole, Round 1

Ch 13. Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc 11, work 3 sc in last ch, then sc 11 down the other side of the chain, work 2 sc in the last ch. Place marker. (28 sts)

Sole, Round 2

Inc, sc 10, inc 3 times, sc 10, inc twice. (34 sts)

Sole, Round 3

Sc in every st around, no increases. (34 sts)

Sides, Rounds 4–12

Sc in every st around for 9 rounds, working through the back loop only on round 4 for a defined edge between sole and sides. (34 sts each round)

Finishing the top opening

Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Weave the tail through the front loops of the last round and pull gently to slightly gather the opening, leaving enough space to fit the foot comfortably. Weave in the end.

Frequently asked questions

How do I size crochet slippers for different feet?

Measure the length of the wearer's foot and add one more round to the sole for each half-inch longer than the base size, or remove a round for a smaller foot.

What yarn makes the warmest crochet slippers?

A wool or wool-blend worsted-weight yarn adds more warmth than acrylic, though acrylic is easier to machine wash and still works well for indoor use.

How do I stop crochet slippers from being slippery on hard floors?

Puffy fabric paint applied in dots to the sole and left to dry fully creates a simple non-slip grip, or purpose-made suede sole patches can be sewn on for a sturdier finish.
