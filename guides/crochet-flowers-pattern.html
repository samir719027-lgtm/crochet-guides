Easy Crochet Flower Pattern (5-Petal, One Skein)

Pin this pattern

A five-petal flower motif you can finish in one sitting — great for pinning onto hats, bags, or hair clips.

Updated 2026 · US terms · 15 min read

This flower is worked in two rounds directly from a magic ring, with the petals added straight into round 1 — there are no separate pieces to sew together, which makes it a fast, satisfying scrap-yarn project.

Quick answer: Round 1 makes a small center ring of stitches, and round 2 builds five petals directly into that ring — no separate parts, no seaming.

What you need

  • A small amount of worsted weight (#4) yarn in your petal color, plus a contrast color for the center (optional)
  • 4.0mm (US G/6) hook
  • Yarn needle
  • Optional: hair clip, brooch pin, or felt backing to mount the finished flower

Round 1: the center

Steps

  1. Make a magic ring and work 10 sc into it. Pull the tail to close the ring. (10 sts)
  2. Slip stitch to join to the first stitch. If using a contrast petal color, fasten off here; otherwise continue directly.

Round 2: the petals

Steps

  1. Join petal-color yarn in any stitch of round 1, if you changed colors.
  2. Chain 3, work 1 dc, 3 tr, 1 dc, chain 3, then slip stitch into that same stitch — this completes one petal.
  3. Skip the next stitch of round 1, then slip stitch into the following stitch.
  4. Repeat the petal (chain 3, dc, 3 tr, dc, chain 3, slip stitch) and skip pattern 4 more times, for 5 petals total.
  5. Fasten off and weave in all ends.

Finishing

Weave in any remaining ends. If the petals curl, block them lightly by pressing under a damp cloth and letting the flower dry flat. Stitch or glue to your finished project.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make the flower bigger?

Yes — start with 12–14 sc in round 1 instead of 10, which leaves more room between stitches for fuller, wider petals.

What if I don't know how to do a magic ring?

See our magic ring tutorial — a small starting chain joined into a ring also works as a substitute.

Can this be turned into a garland?

Yes — make several flowers, then join them with a chain of your desired length between each one.
