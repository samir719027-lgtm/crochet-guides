Free Crochet Plant Hanger Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A simple net-style pouch and hanging cords, built from the same flat circle base as our coasters.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Fits a 5–6in / 13–15cm pot

This hanger starts as a flat circle base, the same increase technique as our coasters pattern, then adds a mesh-style body and hanging cords. It's a sturdy, practical project for cotton yarn and a great way to display a small houseplant.

Materials

  • 2 skeins worsted-weight cotton yarn (about 150–200 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends
  • A sturdy ceiling hook, for hanging the finished piece

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means

inc2 sc in same stitch (increase)

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 5.0mm hook and worsted cotton. A firmer tension than usual helps this piece hold weight without stretching once a pot is loaded.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked in a continuous spiral for the base — same technique as our coasters pattern.
  • Cotton yarn is strongly recommended for strength; acrylic can stretch under the weight of a loaded pot over time.
  • Test the hanging cords with the empty pot before adding soil and the plant, to check the join points hold securely.

Pattern

Base, Round 1

Make a magic ring, 6 sc into ring. (6 sts)

Base, Round 2

Inc in every st around. (12 sts)

Base, Round 3

[sc 1, inc] around. (18 sts)

Base, Round 4

[sc 2, inc] around. (24 sts)

Sides, Rounds 5–12

Sc in every st around, no increases, for 8 rounds to form the sides of the pouch. (24 sts each round)

Hanging cords

Cut 3 lengths of yarn, each about 60in / 150cm long. Fold each in half and attach one at three evenly spaced points around the top edge of the pouch (roughly every 8 stitches), pulling the folded loop through a stitch and threading the ends through to secure.

Finishing

Braid or twist each pair of cord ends together for several inches, then gather all three cords at the top and tie a secure overhand knot to form the hanging loop. Weave in any remaining ends.

Frequently asked questions

What size pot fits this crochet plant hanger?

This pattern's net pouch comfortably holds a pot 5 to 6 inches (13-15cm) in diameter. For a larger pot, add more rounds to the base circle before starting the sides.

What yarn is sturdy enough to hold a plant pot?

Cotton yarn, held double or worked at a tight gauge, is recommended over single-strand acrylic, since it needs to bear the weight of a pot and soil without stretching out over time.

How do I hang a crochet plant hanger safely?

Use a sturdy ceiling hook rated for the combined weight of the pot, soil, and plant, and check the hanging cords periodically for wear, especially if the plant is heavy or the hanger sees direct sunlight, which can weaken some fibres over time.
