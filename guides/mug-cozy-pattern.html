Free Crochet Mug Cozy Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A flat strip that wraps around a mug and closes with a single button — a small project with a real everyday use.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Fits a standard mug

This mug cozy is a flat strip worked in rows, the same basic technique as our headband pattern, closed with a button instead of a twist. It's small enough to finish in one sitting and genuinely useful on your own desk or as a quick gift.

Materials

  • Small amount of worsted-weight yarn (about 20–30 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • 1 button, about 3/4in / 2cm
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends and sewing on the button

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
hdcHalf double crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

12 hdc and 10 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 5.0mm hook and worsted yarn. Gauge isn't critical here; a cozy slightly larger or smaller still works around most standard mugs.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, worked flat in rows — same base technique as our headband pattern.
  • Sized for a standard mug about 3.25–3.5in / 8–9cm in diameter; add a few stitches to the foundation chain for a larger mug.
  • A genuinely quick project, and a good way to use up small leftover amounts of yarn.

Pattern

Foundation

Chain 22.

Row 1

Hdc in the 3rd chain from the hook, and in each chain across. Turn. (20 sts)

Rows 2–4

Ch 2 (counts as first hdc), hdc in each stitch across. Turn. (20 sts each row)

Row 5: buttonhole

Ch 2, hdc 8, ch 2 (skip next 2 sts, leaving a small gap for the button), hdc in remaining 8 sts to end. Turn. (18 sts plus the 2-chain gap)

Row 6

Ch 2, hdc in each stitch across, working hdc into each of the 2 chain sts from the buttonhole gap as if they were regular stitches. (20 sts)

Finishing

Fasten off, leaving a 6in / 15cm tail. Weave in the starting end. Wrap the strip around the mug to find the correct button placement, then sew the button on securely with the remaining tail.

A demonstration of crocheting a coffee mug cozy, useful for seeing the button closure and handle gap placement.

For a larger mug: add 4 more chains to the foundation (in multiples of 2) and adjust the buttonhole row position roughly to the centre of the strip's total length.

Frequently asked questions

What size mug does this cozy fit?

This pattern fits a standard mug about 3.25 to 3.5 inches (8-9cm) in diameter. For a larger mug, add a few extra stitches to the foundation chain.

How much yarn do I need for a mug cozy?

A single mug cozy uses roughly 20 to 30 yards of worsted-weight yarn, easily within a small remnant from another project.

Can I felt a crochet mug cozy for extra insulation?

Yes, if worked in 100% wool yarn, a finished cozy can be lightly felted by hand-washing in hot water and agitating, which thickens the fabric and adds extra heat insulation.
