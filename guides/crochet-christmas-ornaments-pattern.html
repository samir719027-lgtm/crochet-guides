2 Easy Crochet Christmas Ornaments (Star & Snowflake)

Pin this pattern

A puff-stitch star and a simple snowflake motif — small, fast, and perfect for using up leftover yarn before the holidays.

Updated 2026 · US terms · 25 min read

Both ornaments below are worked flat from a magic ring in one sitting, then lightly stiffened so they hold their shape for hanging.

Quick answer: Both ornaments start from a magic ring and finish in under 30 minutes; stiffening with diluted glue afterward keeps them from drooping on the tree.

What you need

  • Small amounts of worsted or DK weight yarn (white, cream, or metallic blends look festive)
  • 3.5–4.0mm hook
  • Yarn needle
  • Ribbon or thread for hanging loops
  • Optional: fabric stiffener or diluted white craft glue

Ornament 1: puff-stitch star

Steps

  1. Make a magic ring and chain 3 (counts as the first stitch).
  2. Work a puff stitch (yarn over, insert hook into the ring, pull up a loop — repeat 4 times in total, then pull through all 9 loops on the hook) into the ring, then chain 1.
  3. Repeat the puff stitch + chain 1 four more times into the ring, for 5 points total, then join with a slip stitch to the top of the first chain-3.
  4. Pull the starting tail to close the center ring tightly.
  5. Optional second round for thicker points: in each chain-1 space, work (slip stitch, chain 2, puff stitch, chain 2, slip stitch), then fasten off.

Ornament 2: simple snowflake

Steps

  1. Make a magic ring and work 12 sc into it, then join with a slip stitch. (12 sts)
  2. Chain 5, slip stitch back into the same stitch to form a small picot loop, then slip stitch into the next 2 stitches of the round.
  3. Repeat the chain-5-picot-and-slip-stitch-2 pattern around for 6 points total.
  4. Fasten off and weave in ends.

Stiffening and hanging

Mix roughly equal parts white craft glue and water, dip each ornament, gently squeeze out the excess, and shape flat on wax paper or a plastic surface to dry overnight. Once dry, thread a small loop of ribbon or thread through one point for hanging.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to stiffen them?

No, but unstiffened ornaments will droop and lose their shape over time — stiffening is optional but recommended for anything hung on a tree.

What's a puff stitch?

A cluster made by pulling up several loops in the same stitch before closing them together, creating a raised, rounded texture — explained step by step above.

Can these be made bigger?

Yes — add an extra round of chain-space stitches around either ornament before fastening off, for a larger finished size.
