Free Crochet Bookmark Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

A tiny flat strip you can finish while your tea is still hot — one of the fastest projects on the site.

Updated 2026 · US terms · Makes 3, about 6in / 15cm long each

This bookmark is a small flat strip, the same basic technique as our dishcloth pattern shrunk right down. It's a genuinely quick project — a good one for using up small leftover bits of yarn from bigger projects.

Materials

  • Small amount of worsted-weight cotton yarn (under 20 yards per bookmark)
  • 4.5mm (US size 7) crochet hook
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends and adding an optional tassel

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)

See our abbreviations glossary for the full list including UK terms.

Gauge

Gauge isn't critical for this small project. Any consistent worsted-weight tension will produce a usable bookmark around 1.5in / 4cm wide.

Notes

  • Written in US terms, using the same flat single crochet technique as our dishcloth pattern.
  • Makes one bookmark; repeat 2 more times for a set of three.
  • A great project for small leftover amounts of yarn from other projects.

Pattern (make 3)

Foundation

Chain 5.

Row 1

Single crochet in the 2nd chain from the hook, and in each chain across. Turn. (4 sts)

Rows 2–24

Chain 1 (does not count as a stitch), single crochet in each stitch across. Turn. (4 sts each row)

Repeat this row until the strip measures about 6in / 15cm, roughly 24 rows for worsted-weight yarn.

Finishing

Fasten off, leaving a 6in / 15cm tail. Weave in the starting end with a yarn needle. Use the remaining tail to add an optional tassel, or weave it in too for a plain finished edge.

A step-by-step demonstration of crocheting a simple bookmark, useful for seeing the stitch count and finishing tassel technique.

Adding a tassel: cut 6–8 strands of yarn about 4in / 10cm long, fold in half, and loop through the bottom edge of the bookmark. Tie tightly at the fold and trim the ends even.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a crochet bookmark take to make?

A single bookmark takes about 15 to 20 minutes once you're comfortable with basic single crochet, making it one of the fastest projects to finish in one sitting.

What yarn is best for a crochet bookmark?

A smooth worsted-weight cotton yarn works well, since it holds a crisp shape and won't snag on book pages the way a fuzzy or textured yarn might.

Can I add a tassel to a crochet bookmark?

Yes, a small tassel made from a few wrapped strands of yarn, tied off and trimmed, is a common and easy addition to the bottom of the bookmark for extra detail.
