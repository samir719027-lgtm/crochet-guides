Free Single Crochet Beanie Pattern for Beginners

Pin this pattern

Your first project with real shaping — a flat crown that grows into a wearable hat.

Updated 2026 · US terms · One adult size, about 21–22in / 53–56cm head circumference

This beanie is where the shaping techniques mentioned in our easiest first projects guide actually happen: a flat circle that grows round by round to form the crown, then straight rounds for the body of the hat. It only uses single crochet and basic increases, no new stitches to learn.

Materials

  • 1–2 skeins worsted-weight yarn (about 150–200 yards)
  • 5.0mm (US size H) crochet hook
  • Stitch marker, to track the start of each round
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle, for weaving in ends

Abbreviations (US terms)

Abbr.Means
chChain
scSingle crochet
st(s)Stitch(es)
inc2 sc in same stitch (increase)
rnd(s)Round(s)

For the full list including UK equivalents, see our abbreviations glossary.

Gauge

14 sc and 16 rows measures about 4in / 10cm square with a 5.0mm hook and worsted yarn. Gauge affects final fit here more than in a scarf — swatch first if you want a precise size.

Notes

  • Written in US terms and worked in a continuous spiral — use a stitch marker to track where each round begins, since rounds are not joined with a slip stitch.
  • This makes one adult size. To adjust: keep increasing the crown by one more round of increases for a larger size, or stop one round earlier for a smaller size.
  • New to amigurumi-style spiral rounds? Our amigurumi guide explains the technique in more depth.

Pattern

Round 1

Make a magic ring, 6 sc into ring, place marker. (6 sts)

Round 2

Inc in every st around. (12 sts)

Round 3

[sc 1, inc] around. (18 sts)

Round 4

[sc 2, inc] around. (24 sts)

Round 5

[sc 3, inc] around. (30 sts)

Round 6

[sc 4, inc] around. (36 sts)

Round 7

[sc 5, inc] around. (42 sts)

Round 8

[sc 6, inc] around. (48 sts)

Rounds 9–20

Sc in every st around, no increases. Work 12 rounds straight, or until the hat measures about 2in / 5cm less than the wearer's head circumference — single crochet fabric stretches once worn.

Finishing

Slip stitch into the next stitch to close, fasten off leaving a 6in / 15cm tail. Weave in ends with a yarn needle.

If your crown ruffles: too many increases were worked for that round. Recount stitches after each increase round against the numbers above before moving on.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know when to stop increasing on a crochet beanie?

Stop increasing once the flat crown circle measures about 2 inches less than the wearer's head circumference, since single crochet fabric stretches slightly once worn.

How much yarn do I need for a crochet beanie?

A standard adult beanie uses roughly 150 to 200 yards of worsted-weight yarn, comfortably within a single 100-gram skein for most brands, with some left over.

Why is my beanie crown coming out ruffled instead of flat?

A ruffled crown means too many increases were worked for the round it's on. Recount your stitches after each increase round and compare to the pattern's stated count before continuing.
